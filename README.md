# Billable

Fast, Powerful Accounting & Inventory Management Software

Take control of your business finances and inventory with Billable—a comprehensive accounting solution designed for modern businesses.

## Why Choose Billable?

- **Lightning-Fast Performance** - Handle your entire accounting workflow without slowdowns
- **Intuitive Interface** - No steep learning curve; get started in minutes
- **Desktop-based** - Work offline with full reliability and security
- **Complete Solution** - All accounting and inventory tools in one place
- **Professional** - Generate invoices, reports, and financial statements instantly
- **Keyboard Accessible** - Fully usable with only the keyboard, no mouse required

## Accessibility

Billable is designed to be fully accessible with keyboard-only navigation:

- **Complete keyboard support** - Every feature accessible without a mouse
- **Global keyboard shortcuts** - F1-F12 and Alt/Ctrl combinations for quick access
- **Focus management** - Clear visual indicators and logical tab order
- **Screen reader friendly** - ARIA labels and semantic HTML throughout
- **Skip navigation** - Jump directly to main content

Press **F1** anytime to see the complete keyboard shortcuts reference. See [KEYBOARD_ACCESSIBILITY.md](KEYBOARD_ACCESSIBILITY.md) for full details.

## What You Can Do

### Manage Your Finances

- Track and record all accounting transactions with ease
- Create and maintain accurate general ledgers
- Generate trial balances and financial reports at any time
- Organize accounts by customizable groups

### Handle Inventory

- Track stock levels and movements across multiple locations
- Manage items, units, and conversion rates
- Record inventory transactions alongside accounting entries
- View real-time stock status reports

### Simplify Invoicing & Sales

- Create professional invoices and sales documents
- Automate standard narrations and descriptions
- Customize document layouts and settings
- Track sales and purchase transactions seamlessly

### Control Your Data

- Backup your data safely and easily
- Freeze data for specific periods for compliance
- Perform housekeeping operations to maintain performance
- Manage multiple company profiles within one application

### Organize Your Business

- Manage multiple companies independently
- Set up your chart of accounts and account groups
- Configure sale and purchase types
- Maintain material centers and distribution points
